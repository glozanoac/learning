# Open-Pit Mining Optimization Studies

The objective of this directory if to cover the gaps of foundational knowledge to be capable of solve complex problems that arise in `open-pit mining operations`.

## LINES OF RESEARCH (TO DEFINE - TENTATIVE)
* Cut-off Grade Optimization (stochastic optimization & Mixed-Integer Programming)
* Real-Time Operational Dispatching & Logistics (stochastic dynamic programming)

## Methodology
1. **Mathematical Foundations:** Developing proofs, understanding convergence behavior, and analyzing algorithm complexity.
2. **Prototyping (Python):** Implement not optimized algorithm with the objective to compute analytical solutions
